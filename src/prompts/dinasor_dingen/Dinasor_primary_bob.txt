### CORE DISAMBIGUATION RULES:
- **[Nutrition vs Substance_use vs Non_LSF]**: Whole foods, nutrients, and oral dietary supplements (e.g., vitamins, minerals, oral OTC melatonin) = `Nutrition`. Recreational drugs, alcohol, tobacco = `Substance_use`. Prescribed clinical medications, synthetic pharmaceuticals, or hospital infusions = `Non_LSF`.
- **[Behavior vs Disease/Outcome]**: Active human lifestyle habits, behaviors, and voluntary practices = `LSF`. Clinical disease endpoints, pathologies, diagnoses, and physiological symptoms (e.g., "insomnia symptoms", "muscle weakness", "cognitive decline") = `Non_LSF`.
- **[Environmental Exposure vs Lab Variable]**: Ambient environmental, occupational, or residential human exposures (e.g., air pollution, secondhand smoke, workplace chemicals) = `Environmental_exposures`. In-vitro reagents, laboratory assay parameters, or animal model experiments = `Non_LSF`.
- **[Physical Activities vs Physiological Signs]**: Voluntary exercise, sports, fitness routines, active transport, and measured physical movement = `Physical_activities`. Passive motor reflexes, clinical gait evaluations, or neurological tremors = `Non_LSF`.
- **[Behavior vs Non-Behavioral Name]**: Behavioral lifestyle expressions = LSF category. Capitalized acronyms or proper nouns denoting clinical trials or research groups = `Non-LSF`.

### BASE RULES (Diagnostic Reference Standard):
Use these rules as the absolute standard when evaluating candidate spans and formulating hints:

1. **Structure & Span Integrity**:
   - *Longest Span:* Entities must capture the maximal meaningful phrase (e.g., "high-fat diet" as one span). Valid multi-word entities are NOT split into fragments or sub-spans.
   - *Conjunction Boundary:* When two LSF phrases are joined by a conjunction (e.g., "and", "or"), they must be treated as two separate entities (e.g., "smoking and alcohol use" -> "smoking" [Substance_use] and "alcohol use" [Substance_use]). The conjunction token is excluded.
   - *Hyphenated Words:* Hyphenated compound words expressing LSFs must be treated as single entity spans (e.g., "smoke-free" -> Substance_use).
   - *Discontinuous Entities:* If an entity is split by non-entity words, fragments are separate.

2. **Affirmative Filtering (Mandatory Exclusions -> Non_LSF)**:
   - *Demographics:* Demographic attributes (age, gender, race, pregnancy, menopause, e.g., "elderly" in "elderly smokers") are NOT labeled as behavioral LSFs (assign Non_LSF unless evaluated strictly under Socioeconomic_factors).
   - *Metabolites / Biomarkers:* Internal physiological biomarkers (e.g., "blood glucose", "serum cortisol", "lipid profile") are NOT LSFs (assign Non_LSF).
   - *Standalone Adjectives:* Standalone adjectives (e.g., "nutritional", "occupational") are NOT LSFs unless part of a specific noun phrase.
   - *General / Generic Terms:* Generic terms (e.g., "carcinogen", "toxin", "lifestyle") are NOT LSFs (assign Non_LSF).

3. **Special Handling Checks**:
   - *Metrics / Scores / Indices:* Terms like "score", "index", "level" that quantify an LSF (e.g., "sleep quality score", "physical activity index") MUST be labeled under the corresponding category.
   - *Medical Procedures:* Only psychotherapy/counseling and consumer cosmetic procedures qualify as LSFs. Other medical, hospital, or pathological surgeries are NOT LSFs (assign Non_LSF).
   - *Context vs. Name Distinction:* Distinguish between a behavioral LSF mention and a non-behavioral reference (study name, acronym, organization).
     * Behavioral (valid LSF): "Participants who smoke..." -> Substance_use.
     * Non-behavioral (study name / acronym): "The SMOKE study group..." -> Non-LSF.

4. **Canonical Category Integrity**:
   - All entity category assignments must strictly be one of the 9 canonical category names (or Non_LSF). Prefix abbreviations (NUT, SEF, ANF) must never be used.

5. **Abbreviation Labeling**:
   - LSF-related abbreviations (e.g., "PA" for Physical_activities) MUST be labeled when appearing in an LSF context.

6. **Specific Category Priority**:
   - Always prioritize specific habit categories (Physical_activities, Nutrition, Sleep, Substance_use) over generic terms whenever specific bodily exertion, dietary intake, or substance use is present.
