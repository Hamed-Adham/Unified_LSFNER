  ## 9 LifeStyle Factors (LSF) Categories:
  > **What:** The nine distinct categories representing all non-genetic health determinants associated with diseases (including nutrition, behaviors, habits, socioeconomic conditions, and environmental exposures) that shape individual health trajectories.
  > **How:** Used to evaluate and compare the Generator's classification decisions against the ground truth annotations to diagnose classification errors (CLSS_ERR) and trace root causes.


  1. **Nutrition** (Diet, nutrients, food patterns): Covers dietary habits, food groups, food processing and preparation, macronutrients, and micronutrients. Includes terms like "caloric intake", "sugar intake", and specific food names.
  2. **Socioeconomic_factors** (Income, education, housing): Includes social and economic conditions such as income, wealth, education, housing, and socioeconomic status.
  3. **Environmental_exposures** (Pollution, hazards): Includes exposure to various environmental factors, such as air pollution, water quality, chemicals (common/consumable), and workplace hazards.
  4. **Substance_use** (Tobacco, recreational drugs): Covers smoking (tobacco), illicit drug use, and recreational substance consumption.
  5. **Physical_activities** (Exercise, occupational movement): Includes regular exercise and physically demanding activities like leisure time, occupational, and household physical activities.
  6. **Non_physical_leisure_time_activities** (Hobbies, screen time): Describes any activity during free time that is not physical, such as screen time, social media usage (if health-related), and hobbies.
  7. **Personal_care_products_and_cosmetic_procedures** (Hygiene, cosmetics): Covers hygiene, use of cosmetic and cleaning products, and invasive procedures like cosmetic surgery or orthodontic treatments.
  8. **Sleep** (Quality, duration): Covers sleep quality, duration, stages, and habits (e.g., "sleep-related", "sleep quality score").
  9. **Mental_health_practices** (Meditation, psychotherapy): Includes behaviors and habits for maintaining mental health and emotional well-being, such as meditation and psychotherapy.

  ## Special Tag:

  **Non-LSF** — Terms that resemble LSFs but are used as study names, organizations, metaphors, or non-behavioral references (labeled O).

  ## Base Rules (Diagnostic Reference Standard):
  > **What:** Core and Primary rules for annotation.
  > **How:** The main standard to analyze the annotations from the Generator and provide structured diagnosis.

  Use these as the absolute standard when diagnosing Generator errors or tagging DynamicGuidelines bullets. Any Generator prediction that violates these rules constitutes an error, regardless of what DynamicGuidelines suggested.

  ### 1. Structure & Tokenization Compliance
  *   **Tokenization:** The Generator must split strictly on whitespace and treat punctuation as separate tokens (e.g., "diet." → ["diet", "."]).
  *   **Longest Span:** Entities must capture the maximal meaningful phrase (e.g., "high-fat diet" as one span). Valid multi-word entities are NOT split into fragments or sub-spans.
  *   **Conjunction Boundary:** When two LSF phrases are joined by a conjunction (e.g., "and", "or"), they must be treated as two separate entities. The conjunction token must be tagged `O`. They are NOT merged into one span (e.g., "smoking and alcohol use" → ["smoking", B-Substance_use], ["and", O], ["alcohol use", B-Substance_use]).
  *   **Hyphens:** Hyphenated words must be treated as single tokens.
  *   **Discontinuous Entities:** If an entity is split by non-entity words in the abstract, the fragments must be labeled separately. Disconnected fragments are NOT merged into one span.

  ### 2. Affirmative Filtering (Mandatory Exclusions)
  The following are NOT labeled as LSFs (they are tagged `O`):
  *   **Demographics:** Demographic attributes (such as age, gender, race, pregnancy, menopause, or puberty terms, e.g., "elderly" in "elderly smokers") are NOT labeled as LSFs (they carry the tag `O`).
  *   **Metabolites / Biomarkers:** Internal biomarkers (e.g., "blood glucose") are NOT labeled as LSFs (they carry the tag `O`).
  *   **Standalone Adjectives:** Standalone adjectives (such as "nutritional" or "occupational") are NOT labeled as LSFs unless they are part of a specific noun phrase.
  *   **General / Generic Terms:** Generic terms (such as "carcinogen") are NOT labeled as LSFs (they carry the tag `O`).

  ### 3. Special Handling Checks
  *   **Metrics / Scores / Indices:** Terms like "score", "index", "level" that quantify an LSF (e.g., "sleep quality score") **must** be labeled.
  *   **Medical Procedures:** Only psychotherapy and cosmetic procedures qualify as LSFs. Other medical procedures are NOT labeled as LSFs.
  *   **Context vs. Name Distinction:** The Generator must distinguish between a behavioral LSF mention and a non-behavioral reference (study name, acronym, organization).
      *   *Behavioral (valid LSF):* "Participants who *smoke*..." → should be labeled with the appropriate LSF category.
      *   *Non-behavioral (study name / acronym):* "The *SMOKE* study group..." → is labeled `Non-LSF` (`O`); it is NOT labeled with a behavioral LSF category.

  ### 4. BIO Tag Integrity
  *   Every `I-Category` **must** follow a `B-Category` or `I-Category` of the **same** category. Orphaned `I-Categories` and category switches mid-span are NOT permitted. (Note: The category in the B- or I-Category must be one of the 9 LSF categories; prefix abbreviations like NUT, SEF, or ANF are NOT used in category names).

  ### 5. Abbreviation Labeling
  *   LSF-related abbreviations (e.g., "PA" for Physical_activities) **must** be labeled when they appear in an LSF context.

  ### 6. Compound / Hyphenated Span Rule
  *   Hyphenated compound words expressing LSFs must be labeled as single spans (e.g., "smoke-free" → `B-Substance_use`).

  # Workflow:

  ## Step 0: Task Restatement

  - Your objective is to diagnose the gap between the Generator's prediction and the ground truth.

  ## Step 1: Gap Analysis → produces `error_identification` and `bullet_tags`

Escape all newlines as \n (represented as \\n in the raw prompt) within the JSON string.
  - **Bullet Evaluation**: Look at the bullet ids used by the generator (provided in "DynamicGuidelines Used by Generator"). Evaluate each bullet ID by examining its performance across the ENTIRE token alignment list (both success cases `TP` / `TN` and error cases `FP` / `FN` / `BIO_ERR` / `CLSS_ERR`):
    - Tag a bullet as **helpful** if it successfully guided the model to make correct predictions (TPs or TNs) in its domain, and did not cause any errors.
    - Tag a bullet as **harmful** if it directly caused, encouraged, or failed to prevent errors (FPs, FNs, BIO_ERRs, CLSS_ERRs) on matching tokens in its domain.

  ## Step 2: Root Cause Identification → produces `root_cause_analysis`

  - Based on the erroneous labels of FP, FN, CLSS_ERR, and BIO_ERR, evaluate the reasoning trace to understand where in the Generator's workflow this error happened (at which specific step).
  - The Generator's workflow consists of the following steps:

    * Step 2: Status Assignment (Behavioral In-context vs Non-LSF).

    * Step 4: Final LSF Decision (applying rules/guidelines).
    * Step 5: Span Boundary Determination and Entity Locking (setting exact token bounds).
    * Step 6: BIO Tag Sequence Construction (mapping B- and I- tags).
    * Step 7: Final Consistency and Integrity Audit.

    - **FP (False Positive):** The Generator model mistakenly considered the present token as an LSF candidate (it shouldn't have done this).

    - **BIO_ERR (BIO Sequence Error):** At the BIO tagging step, the sequence logic had issues and the BIO sequencing rule was not applied correctly.
  - Write your findings as the value of `root_cause_analysis`.
